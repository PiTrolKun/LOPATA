# Uncensored Studio adapters

Rank-64 adapters extracted from community fine-tunes of Z-Image Turbo against
the plain base (Comfy-Org/z_image_turbo, bf16): low-rank SVD of the weight
difference over the 30 transformer layers (attention q, k, v, out; feed-forward
w1, w2, w3; adaLN modulation). Norm and bias changes are not carried. They let
a checkpoint's look run on a hosted Z-Image Turbo adapter endpoint, pay per
image. Use at scale 1.0, 8 to 9 steps, guidance 0.

All credit for each fine-tune goes to its author; each file's metadata names
its source. Extraction script: scripts/extract-zimage-adapter.py in the
Uncensored Studio repository (https://uncensored-studio.art).

separate from the fine-tune; bf16 sources give far higher capture. Prefer the
bf16-derived files where one exists. The v4 adapter lives at
forbiddenmichael/cyberrealistic-zit-v4-adapter.
