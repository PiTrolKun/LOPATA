Living Arcade additive art library / Дополнительная библиотека «Живой аркады»
Catalog revision: 2026-11-06.1

CC0 originals: Glitch (Tiny Speck; SVG conversion bart), 8-Bit Dungeon
(Jamie Cross), Shape Characters (Kenney), Blue Bird (mirex), Cartoon Jester
(laetissima), Cartoon Vector Characters (laetissima).
Original files, author notices, source pages, the CC0 legal code and download
hashes accompany the library in Assets/LivingArcade/Library/licenses and sources.
Prepared PNGs and Kenney assemblies are adaptations; originals remain unchanged.
Glitch logos/trademarks and imitations of proprietary game characters are excluded.

Selected original tank and dragon drawings were generated in LOPATA's local
research with Gemma 4 26B A4B Instruct QAT (Unsloth UD-Q4_K_XL). Their actual
model revision and provenance accompany each catalog entry and prepared PNG.
These generated drawings are not attributed to the CC0 authors or marked CC0.
Gemma model terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms
