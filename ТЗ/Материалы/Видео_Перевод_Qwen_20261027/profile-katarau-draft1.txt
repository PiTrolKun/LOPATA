A narrative music video set in a gloomy, tense atmosphere, with no supernatural beings or direct horrors shown. The main character is a man walking through the streets of an abandoned old city shrouded in thick fog. He feels an inner unease and intuitively seems to be looking for someone, though he doesn’t realize exactly who it is. At the end of his journey, he finds one house, goes inside, and discovers a light burning in the window of an empty room. The scene emphasizes feelings of loneliness, mystery, and an inexplicable presence, leaving the viewer with a sense of uneasy puzzlement.
