A narrative music video set in a dark, tense atmosphere, without supernatural entities or outright horror. The protagonist is a man wandering through the streets of an abandoned old city, shrouded in thick fog. He feels inner unease and intuitively searches for someone, although he himself does not realize who exactly has become the object of his search. At the end of his journey, he finds one house, enters it, and discovers a burning light in the window of an empty room. The scene emphasizes a sense of loneliness, mystery, and an inexplicable presence, leaving the viewer with a feeling of anxious bewilderment.
