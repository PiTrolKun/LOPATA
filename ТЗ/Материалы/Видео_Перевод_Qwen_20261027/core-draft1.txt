Story video in a dark, tense atmosphere without supernatural creatures or direct horror elements. The main character is a man walking through the streets of a deserted old city, hidden behind thick fog. He feels inner unease and intuitively searches for someone, though he himself does not realize who exactly has become the object of his search. At the end of his journey, he finds one house, enters it, and discovers a glowing light in the window of an empty room. The scene emphasizes the feeling of loneliness, secrecy, and an inexplicable presence, leaving the viewer with a sense of anxious confusion.
